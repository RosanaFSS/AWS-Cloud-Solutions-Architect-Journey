<h2  align="center" class="bold" style="font-weight: bold"> AWS Identity and Access Management (IAM)</h2>
<p align="center">AWS Identity and Access Management (IAM) is a service provided by Amazon Web Services (AWS) that enables secure control over access to AWS resources. With IAM, you can create and manage identities, such as users and groups, and grant specific permissions to access services and resources. It plays a crucial role in cloud security by protecting against internal and external threats and ensuring appropriate access to AWS resources.</p>

<h2  align="center" class="bold" style="font-weight: bold">Content</h2>
<p align="center">In this laboratory, you will familiarize yourself with the AWS Identity and Access Management service and its console.</p>
