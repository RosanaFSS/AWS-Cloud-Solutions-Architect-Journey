<h2  align="center" class="bold" style="font-weight: bold">AWS IAM, Creating a Password Policy</h2>
<p align="center">The IAM (Identity and Access Management) Password Policy in AWS plays a crucial role in securing cloud resources. This policy establishes guidelines and requirements for the creation and management of robust passwords for users within an AWS account. With a focus on preventing unauthorized access, the policy defines criteria such as minimum password length, the use of special characters, numbers, and uppercase letters. Additionally, it enforces the practice of regular password updates to mitigate security risks.</p>
